Canon Printer Not Connecting to Computer: Easy Fixes

Is your Canon printer not connecting to your computer? A Canon printer may fail to connect because of WiFi problems, a USB connection issue, incorrect printer settings, outdated software, or a communication problem between the printer and computer.

This guide explains simple ways to troubleshoot Canon printer connection problems on Windows and Mac computers using WiFi or USB.

Why Is My Canon Printer Not Connecting to My Computer?

There are several possible reasons why a Canon printer is not detected by a computer. The printer may be disconnected from the network, connected to a different WiFi network, using a damaged USB cable, or missing the required printer software.

Common causes include:

  • Canon printer is not turned on.
  • Printer and computer are connected to different WiFi networks.
  • WiFi connection is weak or interrupted.
  • USB cable is loose or damaged.
  • The wrong printer is selected.
  • Printer drivers are missing or outdated.
  • The printer is offline.
  • A previous print job is stuck.
  • Printer software is not installed correctly.
  • Computer or printer network settings need to be refreshed.

How to Fix a Canon Printer That Won't Connect to Computer

1. Make Sure the Printer Is Turned On

First, check that your Canon printer is powered on. Make sure the power cable is connected securely and that the printer display or power indicator is active.

If the printer does not turn on, check the electrical outlet and power connection before troubleshooting the computer connection.

2. Restart the Canon Printer

Restarting the printer can clear temporary connection problems.

  1. Turn off the Canon printer.
  2. Wait several seconds.
  3. Turn the printer back on.
  4. Wait until it finishes starting.
  5. Try connecting it to the computer again.

3. Restart the Computer

Temporary operating-system or printer-service problems can sometimes prevent the computer from detecting the printer. Restart your Windows PC or Mac and then check the printer connection again.

4. Check the WiFi Connection

If you are using a wireless Canon printer, make sure the printer is connected to the correct WiFi network.

Your computer should normally be connected to the same local network as the printer. If the printer is connected to another network, the computer may not be able to find it.

For wireless setup instructions, see: Canon Printer WiFi Setup.

5. Check Whether the WiFi Password Changed

If you recently changed your WiFi password or replaced your router, the printer may still have the old network information saved.

Reconnect the Canon printer to the current WiFi network and then check whether your computer can detect it.

6. Check the USB Cable

If your Canon printer is connected through USB, make sure the cable is securely connected to both the printer and computer.

  • Disconnect the USB cable.
  • Reconnect it securely.
  • Try another USB port on the computer.
  • Check the cable for visible damage.
  • Restart the printer and computer.
  • Check whether the printer appears in printer settings.

7. Check the Printer Settings in Windows

On Windows, check the installed printers and make sure your Canon printer appears in the printer settings.

If the printer is listed, check its status and confirm that it is not offline or paused.

If it does not appear, check the WiFi or USB connection and then try adding the printer again.

8. Check the Printer Settings on Mac

On a Mac, open the printer settings and check whether the Canon printer appears in the available printer list.

If the printer is missing, verify the WiFi or USB connection and check whether the appropriate Canon printer software is installed.

9. Check the Printer Driver

A printer driver helps the operating system communicate with the printer. If the driver is missing, outdated, or incorrectly installed, the computer may not communicate with the Canon printer properly.

Check your exact Canon printer model and use the manufacturer's official resources when downloading drivers or software. Choose software that matches your printer model and operating system.

10. Check if the Canon Printer Is Offline

A printer that appears offline may not communicate correctly with the computer. Check the printer status in your computer's printer settings.

If your Canon printer is offline, reconnect it to WiFi or USB and check its status again.

See our detailed guide: Canon Printer Offline: How to Fix It.

Canon Printer Not Connecting to Computer via WiFi

Wireless connection problems are common when the printer and computer are not communicating over the same network.

  1. Make sure the Canon printer is turned on.
  2. Check the printer's WiFi connection.
  3. Make sure the computer is connected to the correct network.
  4. Restart the router.
  5. Restart the printer.
  6. Restart the computer.
  7. Check the printer in your computer's printer settings.
  8. Try adding the printer again if necessary.

If the printer cannot connect to WiFi at all, read: Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi .

Canon Printer Not Connecting to Computer via USB

If your Canon printer uses a USB connection, check the physical connection first. A loose cable or faulty USB port can prevent the computer from detecting the printer.

Try another USB port and reconnect the printer. If the computer still does not detect it, restart both devices and check the printer software and driver.

Canon Printer Connected but Computer Cannot Find It

Sometimes the Canon printer is successfully connected to WiFi but does not appear on the computer. This can happen when the computer and printer are using different networks or when the printer has not been added correctly.

  • Confirm that both devices use the same local network.
  • Restart the printer.
  • Restart the computer.
  • Restart the router if necessary.
  • Check the printer settings.
  • Remove an incorrect printer entry if necessary.
  • Add the Canon printer again.

Canon Printer Not Detected by Windows

If Windows does not detect your Canon printer, first check the physical or wireless connection. Then check whether the printer appears in the Windows printer settings.

If it does not appear, verify the printer driver and software. Make sure you are using software intended for your exact printer model and Windows version.

Canon Printer Not Detected by Mac

If your Mac cannot find the Canon printer, check the WiFi or USB connection. Make sure the printer is turned on and connected correctly.

Then check the Mac printer settings and verify that the Canon printer has been added correctly.

Check the Print Queue

A stuck print job can sometimes make the printer appear unresponsive. Open the printer's queue and check for pending documents.

Cancel stuck documents and then try sending a new print job.

Try Removing and Adding the Printer Again

If the printer is listed but cannot communicate with the computer, removing the printer from the operating system and adding it again may refresh its connection settings.

Before doing this, make sure you know the correct printer model and that the printer is connected through WiFi or USB.

Restart the Router

If your Canon printer uses WiFi, restarting the router can resolve temporary network communication problems.

After the router restarts, allow the printer and computer to reconnect to the network before testing the printer again.

Canon Printer Not Connecting After a Network Change

A new router, changed WiFi password, changed network name, or modified network settings can cause the printer to lose its previous connection.

Reconnect the printer to the current wireless network and then check the computer's printer settings.

Quick Canon Printer Connection Checklist

  • Printer is powered on.
  • Printer has no visible error.
  • WiFi connection is active.
  • Computer is connected to the correct network.
  • USB cable is securely connected if using USB.
  • Correct Canon printer is selected.
  • Printer is not offline.
  • Print queue does not contain stuck documents.
  • Printer driver is installed correctly.
  • Printer software matches the printer model.
  • Printer and computer have been restarted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Canon printer not connecting to my computer?

The problem may be caused by a WiFi connection, USB cable, printer status, driver, software, or incorrect printer settings. Check the connection and restart both the printer and computer.

Why is my Canon printer connected to WiFi but not detected by my computer?

Make sure the printer and computer are connected to the same network. Restart both devices and check whether the printer has been added correctly to the computer.

Why is my Canon printer not connecting through USB?

Check the USB cable and USB port. Reconnect the cable, try another USB port, restart the printer and computer, and check whether the appropriate printer software and driver are installed.

How do I connect my Canon printer to my computer?

You can generally connect a Canon printer through WiFi or USB, depending on the printer model. Follow the setup instructions for your specific model and operating system.

Why does Windows not detect my Canon printer?

Windows may not detect the printer because of a connection problem, missing software, an incorrect printer entry, or a driver issue. Check the printer connection and printer settings first.

Why does my Mac not find my Canon printer?

Check the printer's WiFi or USB connection, confirm that the printer is turned on, and verify that the Canon printer has been added correctly in the Mac printer settings.

What should I do if my Canon printer still will not connect?

Check the power, network or USB connection, printer status, driver, software, and computer settings. If the problem continues, consult the documentation and support resources for your specific Canon printer model.

Important: Printer connection steps can vary between Canon models and operating systems. Always use instructions that match your specific printer model and computer.
