Want to connect your Canon printer to WiFi? This guide explains how to connect a compatible Canon wireless printer to your WiFi network using simple step-by-step instructions. It also covers WPS, Windows, Mac, and common wireless connection problems.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi

Connecting a Canon printer to WiFi allows computers, phones, and other compatible devices on the same network to communicate with the printer without requiring a direct USB connection.

The exact wireless setup steps can vary depending on the Canon printer model. However, the general process involves turning on the printer, opening its wireless setup option, selecting your WiFi network, entering the password, and completing the connection.

What You Need Before Connecting Your Canon Printer to WiFi

Before starting the wireless setup, keep the following information ready:

  • Your Canon printer should be turned on.
  • Your wireless router should be working correctly.
  • You should know your WiFi network name.
  • You should know the current WiFi password.
  • The printer should be within a reasonable range of the router.
  • Your computer or mobile device should be available if needed for setup.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi Step by Step

Step 1: Turn On the Canon Printer

Turn on your Canon printer and wait for it to finish starting up. If the printer displays an error, resolve the displayed issue before beginning wireless setup.

Step 2: Open Wireless Setup

Open the wireless, network, or WiFi setup option on your Canon printer. The name and location of this option can vary by model.

Step 3: Search for Available WiFi Networks

Select the option to search for available wireless networks. The printer should display nearby WiFi networks that it can detect.

Step 4: Select Your WiFi Network

Find your home or office WiFi network in the list and select it.

Step 5: Enter the WiFi Password

Enter your current WiFi password carefully. Check uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters before confirming.

Step 6: Complete the Wireless Connection

Confirm the wireless settings and wait for the printer to connect. Once the connection is successful, the printer may display a connection confirmation or wireless indicator.

Tip: Keep the printer close to the router during initial setup. You can move it to its normal location after confirming that the wireless connection works.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi Using WPS

Some Canon printers and wireless routers support WPS. WPS can provide an alternative way to establish a wireless connection without manually entering the WiFi password.

  1. Turn on the Canon printer.
  2. Open the wireless or network setup option on the printer.
  3. Select the WPS connection option if available.
  4. Press the WPS button on your compatible wireless router.
  5. Follow the instructions displayed by the printer.
  6. Wait for the wireless connection to complete.
Important: WPS availability and setup steps vary by printer and router model. Use WPS only when it is supported by your equipment.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Windows

First connect the Canon printer to your wireless network. Then make sure your Windows computer is connected to the same WiFi network.

  1. Connect the Canon printer to WiFi.
  2. Connect your Windows computer to the same network.
  3. Open Windows printer settings.
  4. Look for the Canon printer in the available printer list.
  5. Select the printer if it is available.
  6. Follow the Windows instructions to complete the printer setup.

If Windows cannot find the printer, check the wireless connection and make sure both devices are using the intended network.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Mac

A Canon wireless printer can be added to a Mac after the printer has successfully connected to the WiFi network.

  1. Connect the Canon printer to your WiFi network.
  2. Connect the Mac to the same WiFi network.
  3. Open the printer settings on the Mac.
  4. Look for the available Canon printer.
  5. Select the printer.
  6. Complete the available setup instructions.

How to Connect Canon Printer to a New WiFi Network

If you changed your router, WiFi name, or WiFi password, your Canon printer may no longer connect automatically to the network.

In this situation, open the printer's wireless setup menu and connect it to the new network using the current WiFi information.

  1. Open the Canon printer's wireless settings.
  2. Search for available networks.
  3. Select the new WiFi network.
  4. Enter the new WiFi password.
  5. Confirm the settings.
  6. Wait for the printer to reconnect.

How to Check Whether Canon Printer Is Connected to WiFi

After completing the setup, check the printer's network or wireless status. Depending on the model, the printer may display a WiFi icon, network information, or a connection status.

You can also check whether your computer can find the printer on the same network and try printing a test document.

Canon Printer Not Connecting to WiFi

If your Canon printer cannot connect to WiFi, check the most common causes first.

  • Verify the WiFi password.
  • Make sure the selected network is correct.
  • Restart the printer.
  • Restart the wireless router.
  • Move the printer closer to the router.
  • Check whether other devices can connect to the WiFi network.
  • Check the printer's wireless settings.
  • Try the wireless setup again.

For more detailed troubleshooting, see our Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi guide.

Canon Printer Connected to WiFi but Not Printing

A printer can sometimes show that it is connected to WiFi while the computer cannot communicate with it correctly.

Make sure the computer and printer are connected to the same network. Also check whether the printer appears offline or whether a print job is stuck in the print queue.

For additional help, visit our Canon Printer Not Printing guide.

Canon Printer Says Offline After WiFi Setup

If your Canon printer was successfully connected to WiFi but Windows or Mac shows it as offline, check the wireless connection and printer status.

Restarting the printer and router can sometimes restore communication. Also make sure the computer is connected to the same WiFi network as the printer.

Canon Printer WiFi Connection Troubleshooting

Quick Checklist

  • Printer is powered on.
  • Router is working normally.
  • Correct WiFi network is selected.
  • Correct WiFi password is entered.
  • Printer is within wireless range.
  • Printer and computer are using the same network.
  • WiFi connection is stable.
  • Printer network settings are correct.
  • Printer has been restarted.
  • Router has been restarted.

Why Is My Canon Printer Not Finding My WiFi Network?

If the printer cannot find your wireless network, check that the router's WiFi is enabled and that the printer is close enough to receive a wireless signal.

If your router broadcasts multiple WiFi networks, carefully check the network names shown by the printer and select the intended network.

Why Does My Canon Printer Keep Disconnecting From WiFi?

Repeated wireless disconnections can be related to signal strength, router issues, network changes, or temporary communication problems.

Move the printer closer to the router and check whether other devices maintain a stable connection. Restarting the printer and router can also help identify temporary connection problems.

Canon Printer WiFi Setup After Changing Router

When you replace your wireless router, the printer may need to be connected to the new WiFi network. The printer may still contain the previous network information.

Open the printer's wireless setup menu and select the new network. Enter the current password and complete the connection process.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Canon printer to WiFi?

Turn on the printer, open the wireless setup option, select your WiFi network, enter the correct password, and complete the connection process.

Can I connect my Canon printer to WiFi using WPS?

If both your Canon printer and wireless router support WPS, you can use the WPS setup method to establish the wireless connection.

Why is my Canon printer not connecting to WiFi?

Common causes include an incorrect WiFi password, weak wireless signal, router problems, incorrect network settings, or a changed WiFi network.

How do I connect a Canon printer to WiFi on Windows?

Connect the printer to WiFi first, connect the Windows computer to the same network, and then add or select the Canon printer in Windows printer settings.

How do I connect a Canon printer to WiFi on Mac?

Connect the Canon printer and Mac to the same WiFi network, then add the printer through the available printer settings on macOS.

How do I connect my Canon printer to a new WiFi network?

Open the printer's wireless setup, select the new WiFi network, enter the current password, and complete the wireless connection process.

Why is my Canon printer connected to WiFi but offline?

The computer may not be communicating with the printer correctly. Check that both devices use the same network and review the printer's connection status.

Conclusion

Connecting a Canon printer to WiFi usually involves selecting the correct wireless network, entering the current password, and completing the printer's wireless setup process.

If the connection does not work, check the WiFi password, router, wireless signal, printer network settings, and connection between the printer and computer. These basic steps can resolve many common Canon printer WiFi connection problems.
