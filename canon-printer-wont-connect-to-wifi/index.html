Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi: How to Fix It

If your Canon printer won't connect to WiFi, the problem may be related to the wireless network, WiFi password, printer settings, router connection, software, or communication between the printer and computer. This guide explains practical steps you can follow to troubleshoot Canon printer WiFi connection problems.

Before you begin:

Make sure your Canon printer is powered on. Keep your WiFi network name and password available, and identify your exact Canon printer model before changing wireless settings or installing software.

Why Is My Canon Printer Not Connecting to WiFi?

A Canon printer may fail to connect to WiFi for several reasons. The printer may be trying to use an old network, the wireless password may be incorrect, the router may have a connection problem, or the printer may be too far from the wireless signal.

Other possible causes include incorrect wireless settings, incompatible software, an incomplete setup process, or a temporary communication problem between the printer and network.

How to Fix a Canon Printer That Won't Connect to WiFi

Follow these troubleshooting steps in order. After each major step, check whether the printer can connect to your wireless network.

Step 1: Make Sure the Printer Is Turned On

Check the Canon printer's power and make sure it has completed its startup process. If the printer displays an error message, resolve the displayed problem before continuing with WiFi troubleshooting.

Step 2: Check Your WiFi Network

Confirm that your wireless router is working correctly. Connect another device, such as a computer or phone, to the same WiFi network to verify that the network is available.

Step 3: Check the WiFi Password

A wrong wireless password can prevent the printer from joining the network. Carefully enter the correct WiFi password during printer setup.

Remember that WiFi passwords can contain uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Step 4: Move the Printer Closer to the Router

If the printer is far away from the wireless router, the signal may be weak. Move the printer closer to the router temporarily and attempt the wireless setup again.

Step 5: Restart the Printer

Turn off the Canon printer, wait briefly, and turn it back on. After the printer finishes starting up, try connecting it to WiFi again.

Step 6: Restart the Wireless Router

Restarting the router can resolve temporary network communication problems. Wait until the WiFi network becomes available again before trying to connect the printer.

Step 7: Check the Printer's Wireless Settings

Open the wireless or network settings on your Canon printer. Confirm that the printer is attempting to connect to the intended WiFi network.

Step 8: Reconnect the Printer to WiFi

If the printer was previously connected to another network, repeat the wireless setup process and select your current WiFi network.

Step 9: Check the Computer's WiFi Connection

If the printer connects to WiFi but the computer cannot find it, verify that the computer is connected to the intended network.

Step 10: Check Canon Printer Software

If the wireless setup software cannot find the printer, verify that you selected the correct Canon printer model and operating system. Use compatible software and drivers from Canon's official resources.

Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi on Windows

If your Canon printer cannot connect to WiFi while using a Windows computer, first verify that the printer and computer can communicate through the intended network.

Check Windows Network Connection

Make sure your Windows computer is connected to the correct WiFi network. If the computer is connected to a different network, printer discovery may not work as expected.

Check Windows Printer Settings

Open Windows printer settings and check whether the Canon printer is already listed. If it is listed but unavailable, review its connection and printer status.

Reinstall the Printer if Necessary

If the existing printer installation is incomplete or incorrect, remove the printer from Windows and add it again using compatible Canon software and the correct connection method.

Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi on Mac

On a Mac, check the printer's wireless connection first. Then verify that the computer is connected to the intended network.

Check the Mac Network

Confirm that the Mac has a working WiFi connection. If the Mac is connected to another network, connect it to the network used by the printer when appropriate.

Check Printer Settings

Open the Mac printer settings and check whether the Canon printer appears in the list of available printers.

Add the Printer Again

If the printer is not available, use the supported printer setup process to add it again.

Canon Printer Connected to WiFi but Computer Cannot Find It

Sometimes the printer successfully connects to WiFi but the computer still cannot find it. This can happen when the computer and printer cannot communicate through the network or when the printer installation on the computer is incomplete.

Check the network connection on both devices. Restart the printer and computer, verify the printer settings, and check the installed printer software.

Canon Printer Cannot Connect After Changing WiFi Password

If you recently changed your WiFi password, the printer may still have the previous wireless credentials stored in its configuration.

Reconnect the printer using the current WiFi network and enter the new password when prompted.

Canon Printer Won't Connect After Changing Router

Replacing your wireless router can require the printer to be configured again. The printer may still be configured for the previous network.

Use the printer's wireless setup process to select the new network and enter the correct password.

Check the WiFi Frequency and Network Compatibility

Some printer models have specific wireless network compatibility requirements. If your printer cannot see the WiFi network at all, check the printer model's documentation and official support information for supported wireless standards and network requirements.

Reset Canon Printer Network Settings

If ordinary troubleshooting does not resolve the connection issue, your printer model may provide an option to reset its network settings.

Network reset procedures vary between Canon printer models. Before using a reset option, review the instructions for your specific printer model because resetting network settings can remove the existing wireless configuration.

Canon Printer WiFi Troubleshooting Checklist

  • Printer is powered on.
  • WiFi router is working.
  • Correct WiFi network is selected.
  • Correct WiFi password is entered.
  • Printer is within suitable wireless range.
  • Printer and computer can communicate through the network.
  • Printer software is compatible with the printer model.
  • Computer is connected to the intended network.
  • Printer and router have been restarted.
  • Printer network settings have been checked.

When Canon Printer WiFi Setup Still Does Not Work

If your Canon printer still cannot connect after completing the basic troubleshooting steps, identify the exact printer model and review its official setup and support instructions.

Different Canon printer models can have different wireless setup menus, supported connection methods, software requirements, and network features. Model-specific instructions are therefore important when general troubleshooting does not solve the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my Canon printer connect to WiFi?

Check the WiFi password, wireless network, printer settings, router, printer location, and compatible software. Restarting the printer and router can also resolve temporary connection problems.

Why can't my Canon printer find my WiFi network?

Check whether the wireless network is operating normally and review your printer model's supported network requirements. Moving the printer closer to the router can also help determine whether signal strength is involved.

Why is my Canon printer connected to WiFi but not detected?

The printer may be connected to WiFi while the computer has an installation or communication problem. Check that the computer and printer can communicate through the intended network and verify the printer software.

How do I reconnect my Canon printer to WiFi?

Open the printer's wireless settings, select the intended WiFi network, enter the current password, and complete the wireless setup process for your printer model.

What should I do if I changed my WiFi password?

Reconnect the printer to your wireless network and enter the new WiFi password. The printer may still contain the previous wireless credentials.

Should I reset my Canon printer's network settings?

A network reset can be useful when the existing wireless configuration is preventing setup, but the procedure varies by model. Review model-specific instructions before resetting the network settings.
